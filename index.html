Community Service Club @ Cornell Tech

The Community Service Club @ Cornell Tech aims to harness the skills and passion of the Cornell Tech campus community to uplift and support the Roosevelt Island community through volunteer service and meaningful community engagement. We organize Community Service Days with local organizations on Roosevelt Island as well as semesterly digital skill and tech support sessions for Roosevelt Island residents.

Oct - Nov 2026
Oct 23 3:30pm - 6pm

Student Affairs Cornell Tech Volunteer Day

Take a break from your studies and give back to the Roosevelt Island community. Cornell Tech is partnering with local organizations including the Roosevelt Island Senior Center, Main Street Theater and Dance, Coler Hospital, Wildlife Freedom Foundation, and more.

Volunteers may garden, organize, and support seniors. A pizza party follows at 6pm for all volunteers.

Organized by the Cornell Tech Student Affairs team. Questions should be directed to Student Affairs.

Oct 24 11am - 3pm

Roosevelt Island Halloween Parade & Extravaganza

Hand out candy to the local community as they exit the parade route and enter the island's annual Halloween celebration.

Where: Southpoint Park, just south of campus.

Stay for as long or as little as you like.

Oct 18 2pm - 4pm

One-on-One Tech Support for Older Adult Residents

Older adult residents of Roosevelt Island visit Cornell Tech with tech support needs ranging from sending email to updating their operating system. Students, staff, and faculty provide personalized one-on-one support.

Where: Tata 141, Cornell Tech.

Volunteer for as long or as little as you want. Coffee and tea will be provided.

More events will be added throughout the semester.
